Computational Fabrication by Multi-Axis 3D Printing

Foundations and Hands-on Practice

A compact course on how curved layers, field-based slicing, material orientation, collision-free toolpaths, inverse kinematics, and machine constraints meet in multi-axis additive manufacturing.

Download PDF See Visuals

Course Snapshot

One design-to-fabrication pipeline, three practical viewpoints.

01

Multi-axis planning

Support-aware fields, curved layers, tool orientations, accessibility, and collision checking.

02

Material-aware paths

Fiber direction, anisotropy, reinforcement layout, and path-driven mechanical performance.

03

Accessible practice

What can transfer to fixed-nozzle desktop non-planar FFF, and what needs richer hardware.

Agenda

3h 45m course format

1h 55m

Foundations: scalar fields, reinforced FDM, S3/Neural Slicer, co-optimization, collision-free planning.

15m

Break: reset between concepts and hands-on planning.

1h 35m

Practice: five-axis planning, IK, three-axis constrained planning, G-code handoff, and discussion.
