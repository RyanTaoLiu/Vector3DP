Agenda

3h 45m course format

Part 1

Introduction. Motivation for moving beyond planar slicing and framing multi-axis printing as a design-to-fabrication pipeline.

Part 1

Field based and support-free planning. Scalar fields, support-aware layer generation, and manufacturable curved-layer decomposition.

Part 1

Anisotropic and reinforcement planning. Path orientation, fiber alignment, and strength-aware deposition for reinforced extrusion.

Part 1

Multi-objectives and co-optimization. S3/Neural Slicer ideas, fabrication fields, structural objectives, and process-aware optimization.

Part 1

Toolpath and collision avoidance. Accessibility, clearance, continuous toolpaths, inverse-kinematics continuity, and support-aware execution.

15m

Break. Transition from foundation and principles to hands-on practice.

Part 2

Slicing for multi-axis motion. Planning curved layers and tool orientations for robot or five-axis platforms.

Part 2

Slicing for 3-axis motion. Fixed-nozzle non-planar FFF constraints and accessible desktop practice.

Part 2

Toolpath and G-code generation. Connect planning outputs to executable paths, G-code export, and hardware handoff.

Part 2

Discussion. Open problems, practical checks, software accessibility, and follow-up project directions.
